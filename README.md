# Ternion

**Ternion** is a beginner-friendly Minecraft modpack designed to introduce players to the three core pillars of modded Minecraft: Technology, Magic, and Adventure. Ternion offers a little bit of everything while still providing a sense of progression toward an end goal.

The pack is developed for Minecraft 1.21.1, using NeoForge as its mod loader

# Pack Overview

Ternion includes a broad selection of mods across technology, magic, and exploration, being a kitchen-sink pack. You are free to engage with the systems that interest you most, without being locked into a single progression path. At the same time, the pack is not aimless: there is one overarching end goal that ties all the major mods together. An extensive quest book acts as your guide throughout the pack. The quests are obviously optional but strongly recommended, especially for players new to modded Minecraft. The pack is also relatively lightweight (currently 260 mods loaded) compared to similarly scoped modpacks, which enables it to run even on lower end hardware.

## Who Is This Pack For?

* Absolute beginners to modded Minecraft
* Players who want to learn cool new ways to do things rather than rush BIS
* Anyone looking for a (mostly) balanced, modern, and guided modded experience

## Requirements

* 4 (maybe 3) GB of RAM allocated to minecraft
* (highly reccomended) minecraft launcher with instance management, such as [Prism](https://prismlauncher.org/)
